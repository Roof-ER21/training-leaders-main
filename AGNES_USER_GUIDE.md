# Agnes 21 Role-Play - User Guide

## Quick Start

1. Navigate to **Module 15: AI Role-Play Simulator** in the sidebar
2. Select your training focus (role)
3. Choose difficulty level
4. Click **Start Training**
5. Read the scenario and type your response
6. Submit to receive scored feedback
7. Progress through multiple scenarios
8. View your session summary

## Difficulty Levels

### Beginner (Pass threshold: 70%)
- **Scenarios:** Basic objections and questions
- **Hints:** More helpful and specific
- **Scoring:** More forgiving keyword matching
- **Best for:** New trainees, first-time practice

**Example:** "I'm busy—just leave a card"

---

### Intermediate (Pass threshold: 70-75%)
- **Scenarios:** Mixed complexity, multi-step responses needed
- **Hints:** Less specific, require critical thinking
- **Scoring:** Standard key point matching
- **Best for:** Trainees with 1-3 months experience

**Example:** "We already had someone look at it"

---

### Advanced (Pass threshold: 75-80%)
- **Scenarios:** Complex, technical, multi-layered
- **Hints:** Minimal guidance
- **Scoring:** Strict rubric adherence
- **Best for:** Experienced reps, team leads

**Example:** "Lead a scope-clarity discussion with an adjuster new to hail claims"

## Scoring System

### How Scores Are Calculated

**70% - Key Point Matching**
- Did you address the expected key points?
- Example: "Acknowledge concern", "Offer two time options"

**30% - Rubric Keywords**
- Did you use important keywords?
- Example: "free", "no obligation", "schedule", "deductible"

**Total Score = (Key Points %) × 0.7 + (Keywords %) × 0.3**

### Score Interpretation

- **80-100**: Excellent - You hit all major points
- **70-79**: Good - Passed, minor improvements needed
- **50-69**: Fair - Missed key points, review feedback
- **0-49**: Needs Work - Review scenario and retry

## Using the Interface

### Voice Input (Microphone Button)
1. Click the microphone icon
2. Speak your response clearly
3. Your speech will be transcribed to the text area
4. Review and edit if needed
5. Submit

**Note:** Voice input requires Chrome, Edge, or Safari. Not available in all browsers.

---

### Hint System
- Click **Get Hint** to reveal a random key point
- Use hints strategically (don't overuse)
- Hints do not affect your score
- Each click shows a different key point

---

### Response Controls

**Submit Response**
- Calculates your score
- Generates AI feedback
- Shows matched/missed points

**Retry This Scenario**
- Clears your response
- Try again with same scenario
- Previous attempt not saved

**Next Scenario**
- Moves to next challenge
- Saves current result
- Cannot go back

**View Session Summary**
- Shows all completed scenarios
- Displays statistics
- Provides recommendations

### Matched Key Points ✅
**What these are:**
- Expected elements that were present in your response
- Critical concepts you successfully addressed

**Example:**
- ✅ Acknowledge and respect decision process
- ✅ Give two time options for both present

---

### Missed Key Points ❌
**What these are:**
- Expected elements that were missing from your response
- Important concepts you should have included

**Example:**
- ❌ Offer brief inspection now for facts
- ❌ Provide simple summary to share

**Pro Tip:** Review missed points and retry the scenario!

---

### AI-Generated Strengths 💪
**What these are:**
- 2 specific things you did well
- Positive reinforcement from Agnes AI

**Example:**
- "You acknowledged their concern respectfully"
- "You offered two specific time options"

---

### AI-Generated Improvements 📈
**What these are:**
- 2 specific areas to enhance
- Actionable feedback for next time

**Example:**
- "Consider mentioning the free/no-obligation aspect earlier"

## Best Practices

### Before Starting
1. Read the scenario carefully
2. Identify the core objection/question
3. Think about expected key points
4. Structure your response (Acknowledge → Educate → Next Step)

---

### During Response
1. **Use the L.E.A.R.N. framework:**
   - **L**isten (acknowledge)
   - **E**mpathize
   - **A**sk clarifying questions
   - **R**espond with value
   - **N**avigate to next step

2. **Include key elements:**
   - Acknowledgment/empathy
   - Value proposition (free, no obligation)
   - Social proof (neighbors, local)
   - Clear next step (two time options)

3. **Use natural language:**
   - Don't just list keywords
   - Sound like a real conversation
   - Be professional but personable

---

### After Feedback
1. Review matched points (celebrate wins!)
2. Analyze missed points (what to add next time)
3. Read AI strengths (what worked)
4. Implement improvements (actionable feedback)
5. Retry if score < 70% (practice makes perfect)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Keyword Stuffing
**Bad:** "I understand, free no obligation schedule today tomorrow neighbors storm deductible"

**Good:** "I totally understand your concern. We offer a free, no-obligation inspection—we've been helping a lot of your neighbors after the recent storm. Would today at 4pm or tomorrow at 10am work for a quick 15-minute check?"

---

### ❌ Ignoring the Role Context
Remember who you're playing:
- **Homeowner role?** You're the rep responding
- **Rep role?** You're practicing delivery
- **Adjuster role?** You're the rep defending scope

---

### ❌ Skipping Key Points
Don't assume the AI knows you meant it—be explicit:
- State the acknowledgment clearly
- Offer specific times (not "later")
- Mention "free" and "no obligation"

---

### ❌ Being Too Vague
**Bad:** "I can come by sometime"

**Good:** "Would 4pm today or 10am tomorrow work for a quick 15-minute inspection?"

---

### ❌ Not Using Hints Strategically
- Use hints if stuck (don't guess blindly)
- Don't overuse hints (defeats learning purpose)
- Read hints before submitting (incorporate feedback)

## Troubleshooting

### Voice Input Not Working
- **Check browser:** Chrome/Edge recommended
- **Check permissions:** Allow microphone access
- **Check audio:** Ensure mic is plugged in
- **Fallback:** Type your response manually

---

### Score Seems Wrong
- **Review matched/missed points:** See what was detected
- **Check for typos:** Keyword matching is case-insensitive but spelling matters
- **Read AI feedback:** May reveal missing elements
- **Retry scenario:** Apply improvements and resubmit

---

### AI Feedback Not Loading
- **Wait a moment:** AI generation takes 2-5 seconds
- **Check API key:** Ensure Gemini API is configured
- **Refresh page:** Reload if stuck
- **Contact support:** If persistent issue

---

### Scenarios Not Progressing
- **Click Submit first:** Must submit before Next Scenario appears
- **Check pool size:** Some role/difficulty combos have fewer scenarios
- **View Summary:** Shows what's completed vs remaining

## Tips for Maximum Learning

### 1. Start with Beginner
Even if experienced, start at beginner level to:
- Learn the scoring system
- Understand expected key points
- Build confidence

### 2. Focus on One Role
Don't jump between roles—master one at a time:
- Complete 5-10 scenarios in one role
- Achieve 80%+ average before switching
- Build muscle memory for that perspective

### 3. Retry Low Scores
If you score below 70%:
- Review missed points carefully
- Use hints to guide your retry
- Incorporate AI improvements
- Resubmit until you pass

### 4. Use Real-World Language
Practice as if it's a real door knock:
- Use contractions (don't, can't, we've)
- Sound conversational
- Avoid robotic/scripted tone

### 5. Time Yourself
Challenge yourself:
- Set a 60-second timer
- Practice quick, concise responses
- Builds door-knocking speed

### 6. Track Your Progress
After each session:
- Note your average score
- Identify patterns (what scenarios you struggle with)
- Set goals (e.g., 80% average next session)

## Session Strategy

### Full Training Session (30-45 minutes)
1. **Warm-up (5 min):** 2-3 beginner scenarios
2. **Main practice (25 min):** 5-7 intermediate scenarios
3. **Cool-down (5-10 min):** Review summary, note improvements
4. **Follow-up:** Practice real-world door knocks with new skills

---

### Quick Practice Session (10-15 minutes)
1. Select role and intermediate difficulty
2. Complete 3 scenarios
3. Quick review of missed points
4. Apply learnings same day

---

### Mastery Session (60+ minutes)
1. Select advanced difficulty
2. Complete all scenarios in one role
3. Retry any below 80%
4. Review detailed summary
5. Create personal improvement plan
