### Commission Structure

**Insurance Sales (Primary):**
- Downpayment: $1,000 flat
- Completion: 6-16% tiered (based on monthly volume)
  - March-October: 10+ signups = 16%
  - November-February: 8+ signups = 16%
  - December-January: 4+ signups = 16%
- First 6 weeks: Automatic 16%

**Retail Sales:**
- Downpayment: $0
- Completion: 2%

**Monthly Bonuses:**
- 15 signups/$200k: $2,000
- 40 signups/$450k: $16,000
- President's Club: $2M annual or $750k in 3 consecutive months

### Escalation Matrix

**Key Rule:** Never escalate without complete documentation

## Value Proposition for Chatbot

### **Problem Solved:**
New sales reps face information overload:
- 95 slides of training content
- 600+ scenarios to remember
- 12+ scripts to master
- 30+ templates to choose from
- 40+ reference documents to know
- Complex escalation protocols
- State-specific regulations

### **Chatbot Solution:**
Instant access to:
- Any training scenario (600+ Q&A)
- Correct template recommendation
- Script guidance by situation
- Escalation advice with documentation requirements
- Commission calculations
- Photo checklists
- Safety protocols
- Code references

### **Impact:**
- **Faster Ramp-Up:** New reps productive in days, not weeks
- **Higher Quality:** Consistent use of proven templates and scripts
- **Better Outcomes:** Proper escalation = more approvals
- **Increased Revenue:** Higher average tickets, faster closings
- **Reduced Errors:** No guessing, instant guidance
- **Improved Safety:** Quick access to safety protocols
- **Manager Time Saved:** Reps self-serve common questions

### **ROI Potential:**
If chatbot helps each rep:
- Close 1 extra deal/month: +$23k revenue × 16% = $3,680 commission
- Increase average ticket 10%: +$2,300 revenue × 16% = $368/deal
- Reduce time to productivity by 2 weeks: 2 weeks × potential deals
- Decrease escalation errors: More approvals = more revenue

For a team of 10 reps:
- 10 extra deals/month: $230k revenue, $36,800 commissions
- Average ticket increase: $23k × 10% × 10 reps × 10 deals = $23k/month
- Total potential impact: $250k+/month in additional revenue
