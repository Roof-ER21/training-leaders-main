## Sales Cycle Deep Dive (9-16 Week Process)

### **Stage 1: Lead Generation (Ongoing)**
- **Activity:** Door knocking
- **Goal:** 70+ doors/day minimum
- **Tools:** Field Portal App for pinning houses
- **Outcome:** Inspection scheduled

### **Stage 2: Initial Inspection (Week 0)**
- **Duration:** 15-20 minutes
- **Activities:**
  - Exterior perimeter damage documentation
  - Roof inspection with ladder
  - Collateral damage photos (gutters, screens, siding)
  - Hail/wind damage identification and marking
  - Granule collection documentation
- **Photos Required:**
  - Mailbox/house number
  - 4 elevation overviews
  - Collateral damage (all elevations)
  - Roof overview
  - Circled hail damage (close-ups)
  - Slashed wind damage (close-ups)
  - Chalked damage overviews
  - Granules in gutters/downspouts
- **Script:** Initial Pitch → Post-Inspection Pitch
- **Outcome:** Homeowner agreement to file claim

### **Stage 3: Claim Filing (Week 0 - Same Day)**
- **Activities:**
  - Collect homeowner information
  - Help homeowner file claim via app/website/phone
  - Sign Contingency Agreement
  - Sign Claim Authorization
  - Submit documents to Field Portal
- **Tools:** Sales App (iPad), Claim Filing Information Sheet
- **Outcome:** Claim filed, adjuster meeting scheduled

### **Stage 4: Adjuster Meeting (Week 1 - Days 2-7)**
- **Timeline:** Insurance schedules within 24-48 hours
- **Pre-Meeting:**
  - Arrive 15-30 minutes early
  - Chalk metals (NOT shingles) day before
  - Confirm homeowner availability
  - Avoid Tuesday mornings (Sales Meetings)
- **During Meeting:**
  - Build rapport with adjuster/ladder assist
  - Walk roof with adjuster
  - Tactfully present evidence
  - Document adjuster statements
- **After Meeting:**
  - Create formal Photo Report
  - Submit Hover report
  - Email Photo Report + Claim Auth to adjuster
  - Update homeowner on next steps
- **Outcomes:**
  - **Estimate Pending:** Adjuster found matching damage
  - **Decision Pending:** Desk adjuster will review
  - **Partial/Denial:** Begin escalation process

### **Stage 5A: Full Approval Path (Week 2-3)**
- **Activities:**
  - Insurance generates estimate
  - Insurance releases ACV (Actual Cash Value) payment
  - Roof-ER office reviews estimate
  - Job assigned to Project Coordinator
  - Project Meeting scheduled (within 1 week)
- **Sales Rep receives:** $1,000 downpayment commission
- **Outcome:** Project Meeting scheduled

### **Stage 5B: Partial/Denial Path (Week 2+)**
- **Activities:**
  - Review what was/wasn't approved
  - Select appropriate template response:
    - Generic Partial Template
    - Siding Argument
    - Repair Attempt Template + Video
    - iTel Template + Discontinued List
    - GAF Guidelines
    - Estimate Request Template
    - Template from Customer to Insurance
  - Submit supplemental evidence
  - Escalate to Team Leader if needed
  - Escalate to Sales Manager if needed
  - File complaint with state regulator if needed
  - Initiate arbitration if needed
- **Resources:** 100+ training scenarios (Q501-Q600), Quick Strike Guide
- **Outcome:** Convert to full approval or close as denied

### **Stage 6: Project Meeting (Week 3-4)**
- **Timeline:** Within days of full approval
- **Conducted by:** Project Coordinator (not sales rep)
- **Activities:**
  - Review project scope
  - Explain product options
  - Sign Project Documents
  - Submit ACV (downpayment)
  - Schedule install date
- **Outcome:** Install scheduled for 4-6 weeks out

### **Stage 7: Installation (Week 7-10)**
- **Timeline:** 4-6 weeks after downpayment
- **Activities:**
  - Production team completes installation
  - Quality control inspection
  - Clean-up and final walk-through
- **Tools:** Trade Calendar for tracking
- **Outcome:** Installation complete

### **Stage 8: Wrap Up & Final Payment (Week 9-12)**
- **Activities:**
  - Quality Check and Wrap Up meeting
  - Homeowner signs Certificate of Completion
  - Certificate submitted to insurance
  - Insurance releases Depreciation payment
  - Homeowner submits Final Payment (Depreciation + Deductible)
- **Sales Rep receives:** Remaining 16% commission (if qualified)
- **Total Timeline:** 9-16 weeks from initial knock to final payment

### **Stage 9: Post-Install (Ongoing)**
- **Activities:**
  - Request referrals
  - Place yard sign
  - Request reviews
  - Add to portfolio
- **Outcome:** Referral leads and testimonials

## Commission Structure Detailed

### **Insurance Sales (Primary Revenue)**

#### **Downpayment Commission:**
- **Amount:** $1,000 flat (paid when homeowner submits ACV/downpayment)
- **Exception:** Townhomes don't count unless:
  - Has aluminum siding, OR
  - Minimum 15 squares

#### **Completion Commission (Tiered):**

**March - October:**
- 10+ Sign-ups: 16% of job value
- 8-9 Sign-ups: 10% of job value
- Less than 8: 6% of job value

**November - February:**
- 8+ Sign-ups: 16% of job value
- Less than 8: 6% of job value

**December - January:**
- 4+ Sign-ups: 16% of job value
- Less than 4: 6% of job value

**First 6 Weeks:**
- Automatic 16% regardless of volume (learning period)
- After 6 weeks, must qualify for 16% tier

### **Retail Sales (Secondary Revenue)**
- **Downpayment:** $0 (no upfront commission)
- **Completion:** 2% of job value
- **Solar:** 2% of job value

### **Monthly Bonuses:**
- 15 Sign-ups OR $200k revenue: $2,000
- 20 Sign-ups OR $250k revenue: $4,000
- 25 Sign-ups OR $300k revenue: $6,000

- Consult teammates

- **Provides:** Review, coaching, intervention
- **Not yet:** Management escalation

- **When:** Team Leader review fails to resolve

- **Provides:** Senior-level carrier communication

- **Not for:** Process failures (use complaint instead)
- **Requires:** Sales Manager approval
- **Tool:** Arbitration Information.docx
